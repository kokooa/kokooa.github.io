Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026.

Your privacy

Bento Bounce does not require a game account. Your game records are stored on your iPhone. Optional rewarded ads use Google's Mobile Ads SDK, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages advertising privacy choices.

Saved on your iPhone

The app stores your best pull counts, last selected lunch, sound and haptics settings, ad-unlocked lunches, and reward receipt tokens on this device. These records remember your progress and prevent the same reward from being granted twice. Apple-managed device backups may include them.

Deleting the app or erasing its data can remove local records; offloading keeps them. A device backup may restore earlier records, but recovery of stars or ad unlocks after reinstalling or changing devices is not guaranteed. The developer does not keep a server copy of these records. Restore purchases restores an eligible Full picnic purchase, not stars or ad unlocks.

Rewarded ads and Google

The app requests non-personalized ads. It does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission or request access to the IDFA. This does not mean that advertising services receive no data.

Google may receive your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app identifiers, ad views and interactions, and crash or performance diagnostics. This information supports ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and service reliability.

UMP handles consent messages and choices where required. When available, use Settings → Advertising privacy options to review those choices. Changing advertising choices does not remove lunches you already unlocked. Google processes advertising data under its own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

Apple processes optional in-app purchases. Bento Bounce uses Apple's verified purchase status to unlock the full picnic. The developer does not receive your payment card details through the app. Apple manages purchase history and refunds under its own policies. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Contact and support

For privacy questions, contact kokooa0183@gmail.com. If you email support, the email service delivers the information you choose to send. Avoid including payment details or other sensitive information. Support messages are used to answer your request; you can ask for them to be deleted.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated.
