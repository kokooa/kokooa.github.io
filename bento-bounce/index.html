How do I play?

Grab a food friend, pull in the opposite direction to your target, and release. A longer pull gives more power. Guide every friend into their matching dish with as few pulls as possible. You can use Undo, Retry, and Hint freely.

What does Full picnic include?

The first 12 lunches are free. You can open the remaining lunches with optional rewarded ads or one Full picnic purchase. Each completed ad permanently opens the next 3 lunches on this iPhone, up to all 28. The final ad opens just lunch 28.

You can choose another ad immediately, with no daily limit and no need to finish a lunch first. Another ad never starts automatically. Closing an ad before earning its reward does not unlock lunches. Ads require an internet connection and may not always be available.

The optional, non-consumable Full picnic purchase permanently unlocks all 28 included lunches without further ad unlocks. There is no subscription. The app displays your App Store region's price before purchase. No more unlock ads are offered once all lunches are open.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase, open Settings → Full picnic, and choose Restore purchases. This restores a Full picnic purchase. It does not restore stars, settings, or ad unlocks. Ad unlocks have no time limit, but recovery after reinstalling or changing devices is not guaranteed.

Can I play offline?

The levels are included in the app, and already-open lunches can be played offline. Rewarded ads require an internet connection. Loading products, buying, and restoring purchases require a connection to Apple's services.

Where is my progress?

Completed lunch records, settings, and ad unlocks are saved on your device. Deleting the app can remove its local data; offloading keeps it. A device backup may restore earlier data, but recovery is not guaranteed. The developer does not keep a server copy of your stars or ad unlocks.

How do I review advertising privacy choices?

Open Settings → Advertising privacy options when that option is available. Google's privacy messages manage applicable consent choices. The app requests non-personalized ads; Google may still process technical and advertising data. See our Privacy Policy.
