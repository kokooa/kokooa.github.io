How Otter Forge handles your game save, purchases, optional ads and support messages.

About this policy

This policy applies to the Otter Forge iPhone game and this support site. “We” means the developer of Otter Forge, reachable at the email above. The game works without a game account. This does not mean that no information is processed: local game data, Apple purchases, Google ad services and emails each have different roles, described below.

Purchases and rewarded ads operate only when available in the app. Features marked “Coming soon” are unavailable in that build.

1. Your game save and notifications

We store progress on your device: weapons and board slots, equipment, stages, upgrades, scrap and core balances, mine production and collection times, daily reward records, tutorial progress, language, sound and notification choices. Purchase transaction IDs, permanent purchase entitlements and random ad reward attempt IDs are also saved locally to apply benefits and prevent duplicate rewards.

The game currently has no developer-operated account, cloud-save service or progress-sync server. It does not upload this save to a developer game server. Device backups may contain app data according to your Apple device and backup settings.

Mine-full notifications are scheduled locally after you enable them and grant notification permission. The game does not register a remote push-notification token for these alerts. It does not ask for access to contacts, the camera, microphone, photo library or precise location.

2. In-app purchases

Apple processes payment through the App Store. The app receives verified purchase information such as the product, transaction ID, quantity, purchase date and entitlement status to deliver purchases, restore permanent benefits and reflect revocations. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details or Apple Account password from these purchases.

Core packs are consumable purchases. The mine auto-collection and ad reward pass are permanent, non-consumable purchases. Restoring eligible permanent purchases is available in Shop → Restore purchases; it is separate from restoring your save or already-delivered consumable cores.

Apple handles its own purchase and account data under App Store & Privacy.

3. Optional ads and privacy choices

When you choose a rewarded ad, Google AdMob delivers it and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages applicable privacy messages and choices. Ad services may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device or advertising identifiers where permitted, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. These support ad delivery, measurement, service improvement and fraud prevention; permitted information may also support personalized ads.

Personalized ads are eligible only within your applicable consent and iOS tracking permissions. An iOS tracking refusal is respected separately from any Google privacy choice. When tracking is not authorized, the app requests non-personalized ads; Google’s consent signals and available demand determine whether a non-personalized or limited ad can actually be served. Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

The app requests ads only when Google’s consent system permits an ad request. It uses a randomly generated reward-attempt ID to associate an ad with its reward; this is not a game-account identifier. We do not send your support email address or local weapon inventory as an ad identifier.

You can play without agreeing to tracking or personalized ads. Agreeing does not increase the reward, and refusing does not reduce the reward for an available completed ad. Ad availability is not guaranteed. Read how Google uses information from partner apps and Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Change your choices

  • Ad choices: use Settings → Ad privacy options when that entry is shown. Google determines when this privacy-options form is required. You may also decline optional ads.
  • Tracking: manage Otter Forge in your iPhone’s Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. System wording and availability may vary. Google privacy choices do not override your iOS tracking choice. Apple explains these controls.
  • Notifications: turn off mine alerts in the game or manage the app’s notification permission in iPhone settings.
  • Support data: email us to request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us. Depending on your location, additional privacy rights may apply. We may need information sufficient to verify the request.

5. Support messages and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments or device/purchase details you choose to include. We use them to respond, investigate problems and handle privacy requests. The support mailbox uses Gmail, so Google also processes the email under its service policies. Please send only what is needed and redact unrelated personal information.

These support pages do not include our own analytics, ad scripts, forms or tracking cookies. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information such as IP address and request logs to deliver and secure the site, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link or sending email uses the separate provider’s service.

6. Retention, deletion and service providers

Local progress remains in app storage while you keep the app and its data. Deleting the app removes its local storage; offloading an app is different and may preserve data. Deletion can permanently remove progress and core balances. Device backups are controlled separately through your device settings. We cannot remotely recover or delete a save held only on your device.

We keep support correspondence as needed to resolve the request, follow up on related issues and meet any applicable recordkeeping obligations. You can request deletion at the contact email. Where some information must be retained, we will explain the reason when responding.

Apple and Google retain information they process under their respective policies and controls. Removing the game or contacting us does not automatically delete their payment, advertising or account records. Use their linked privacy information for provider-specific requests. These providers may process information in countries other than your own under the arrangements described in their policies.

7. Questions and changes

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Otter Forge developer at kokooa0183@gmail.com. A parent or guardian with concerns about information a child has shared can use the same contact. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information in the initial message.

We will update this page when these practices change and revise the date above. The game supports English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese; this policy is currently provided in English and Korean.
