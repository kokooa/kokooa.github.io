Common questions

How do merging and currencies work?

Open Merge and drag a weapon onto another weapon of the same level, or select the two slots in sequence. The board has 24 slots. Scrap pays for combat upgrades; cores pay for weapon crafting and mine upgrades.

Each craft costs 1 core. Level odds are 80% / 18% / 2% for the displayed base level / base + 1 / base + 2. Sword and axe are independently 50% each. Check the current odds in Merge or Shop → Weapon crafting odds before spending cores.

How do I restore a permanent purchase?

Use the Apple Account that bought it, open Shop, scroll down and tap Restore purchases. This restores eligible permanent mine auto-collection and ad reward pass purchases. It does not recreate already-delivered consumable cores or a lost game save.

If a completed purchase has not appeared, keep the app installed, check your connection, reopen the app and contact support if the issue continues. Do not buy it again to troubleshoot.

What do the two permanent products do?

Permanent Mine Auto-Collect collects the cores your mine produces and prevents production from stopping at the six-hour manual storage cap. It does not increase the mining rate.

Permanent Ad Reward Pass lets you claim the same 6-core reward without a video, up to twice a day. It shares the same daily claim limit as rewarded ads and does not add extra claims. Both are one-time purchases, not subscriptions.

Why did my mine stop producing?

Manual storage holds up to six hours of production. Open Mine and collect the stored cores to make room. Mine upgrades use cores and increase production. Mine-full alerts are optional: enable them in Mine and allow notifications in iPhone settings.

Do I have to accept personalized ads?

No. You can continue playing without accepting ad personalization or tracking. If an ad allowed by your choices is available, its reward conditions are unchanged. Ad availability depends on your privacy choices, region, connection and ad supply; an ad is not always available.

When shown, use Settings → Ad privacy options to revisit ad choices. iOS tracking permission is managed separately in iPhone privacy settings. See the privacy policy for details.

What if an ad reward is missing or a button says “Coming soon”?

A rewarded ad must reach the provider’s reward-completion event; simply opening or closing it does not grant cores. If a reward is missing after completion, contact support with the time and what you saw. “Coming soon” means that purchase or ad is not currently available in your build. Avoid reinstalling to fix it.

Can I move my progress to another device?

Progress and core balances are stored on your device. The game does not currently offer its own account, cloud save or cross-device progress sync. Removing the app can erase that save. Apple purchase restoration for permanent benefits is separate from restoring progress or consumable balances.

How do I mute the game or request a refund?

For game effects, turn off Settings → Sound effects. If you need a refund for an Apple purchase, use Apple’s refund request instructions. Apple decides refund eligibility.
